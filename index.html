Welcome to the RE2R Wiki

Resident Evil 2 (2019) is a modern reimagining of Capcom’s classic survival‑horror game, rebuilt with new visuals, expanded storylines, and updated gameplay while staying true to the spirit of the 1998 original. Players follow Leon S. Kennedy and Claire Redfield as they fight to survive a catastrophic viral outbreak in Raccoon City, uncovering the sinister truth behind the Umbrella Corporation. The remake blends atmospheric exploration, tense combat, and cinematic storytelling to deliver a fresh yet faithful experience for both longtime fans and newcomers.
