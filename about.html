Purpose of this site

This fan-made wiki is a quick reference for Resident Evil 2 (2019). It brings useful game information together in one place, so players can explore the story, characters, and extras.

  • Browse profiles for the people caught up in the Raccoon City outbreak.

Spoiler notice: some pages may reveal story events, character details, or unlock requirements. Take care if you are playing for the first time.

About Resident Evil 2

Released in 2019, Resident Evil 2 is Capcom's reimagining of the 1998 survival-horror game. The story follows rookie police officer Leon S. Kennedy and college student Claire Redfield as they try to survive the outbreak in Raccoon City.

Leon and Claire each have their own campaign, locations, and encounters. Exploring the city, solving puzzles, managing limited supplies, and facing dangerous creatures are all central to making it through the night.
