Leon S. Kennedy

Character details

Starting weapon: Matilda, a 9mm handgun with a burst-fire upgrade. Other weapons: Finds and upgrades weapons including the W-870 shotgun and Lightning Hawk magnum. Gameplay: Leon's campaign features the RPD, sewers, and NEST laboratory, with puzzles and limited supplies to manage. Health: His condition is shown as Fine, Caution, or Danger. Healing items restore health; the game does not show a numeric HP total.
